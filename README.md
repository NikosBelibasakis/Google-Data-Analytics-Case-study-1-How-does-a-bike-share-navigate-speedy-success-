# Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study

## Google Data Analytics Capstone Project

This repository contains the implementation of the Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study, developed as the final capstone project of the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate.

The purpose of the project was to apply the complete data analysis workflow in a real-world business scenario using the six phases of the Google Data Analytics process: Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share, and Act.

The case study focuses on the analysis of Cyclistic bike-share trip data from the first quarter (Q1) of 2019 and 2020. The main business objective of the project was to investigate the behavioral differences between casual riders (Customers) and annual members (Subscribers), in order to support strategies aimed at converting more Customers into Subscribers.

- During the Ask phase, the business problem, objectives, stakeholders, and research questions of the case study were identified and analyzed. 
- In the Prepare phase, the datasets were collected, organized, examined, and evaluated in terms of structure, integrity, and reliability.
- The Process phase focused on data cleaning, preprocessing, transformation, schema standardization, invalid data removal, and the unification of the datasets into a final dataset named "Divvy_Trips_final", containing approximately 750,000 valid ride records ready for analysis.
- During the Analyze phase, statistical analysis, pivot tables, and data visualizations were used to identify behavioral patterns and differences between Customers and Subscribers. The analysis included ride duration analysis, ride frequency analysis by day of week, and the identification of the most popular start and end stations.
- In the Share phase, the findings and visualizations were organized and presented in a structured and understandable way.
- Finally, in the Act phase, business recommendations and strategic proposals were formulated based on the analysis results.

Tools and techniques used throughout the project include:

* Microsoft Excel
* Google Sheets
* Pivot Tables
* Data Cleaning Techniques
* Data Transformation and Preprocessing Techniques
* Data Visualization Techniques

This project serves as a practical example of how data analysis can be applied to support data-driven decision making and business strategy development in a real-world environment.
